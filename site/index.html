How a day works

Learn it. Practise it. Keep it.

Every day follows the same simple rhythm, so you always know what to do when you open the app.

  1. 1

    Check in

    Rate how you feel before the lesson — 1 to 5. You'll rate again at the end and see the difference.

  2. 2

    Learn one skill

    A short guided lesson in clear steps, with flashcards and a quick quiz to make it stick.

  3. 3

    Take on a practice

    Finishing the lesson unlocks small real-life habits you repeat over the next days.

  4. 4

    Come back tomorrow

    The next lesson unlocks the next day. One step at a time is how charisma becomes natural.

Your toolkit

Tools you keep, for the moments that matter

Some lessons unlock a reusable tool. Open it anytime — before a meeting, a date or a difficult call.

  • Presence Reset

    A guided breathing reset — in for 4, out for 6 — for 1, 3 or 5 minutes. Unlocks on Day 1.

  • Grounding Reset

    A short reset for anxiety, tension or overwhelm: 5-4-3-2-1 senses, release tension, name the feeling, or STOP. Unlocks on Day 5.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Charisma30 really free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no ads.

How much time does it take each day?

One short lesson plus small practices you fit into your normal day. Most people spend a few minutes on the practices and around a quarter of an hour on the lesson.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Your next lesson waits for you — you simply continue where you left off. Your streak restarts, your progress doesn't.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your name, progress and habits are stored only on your phone.

Which languages is the app in?

English, Italian and French. You can switch language at any time in Profile → Language.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Charisma30 does not upload your data, and uses no analytics or advertising services. You can erase everything at any time from Profile → Reset Progress.
