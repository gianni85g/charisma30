Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Your privacy is important. This policy explains how Charisma30 handles your data.

1. Data storage

All progress (completed days, name, avatar, habits and so on) is stored locally on your device. No data is uploaded to external servers.

2. Personal information

The app does not collect or transmit your personal information. Any name you enter is stored only on your device.

3. Third-party services

Charisma30 does not use analytics tools, advertising frameworks or external tracking services.

4. Deleting your data

You can erase all stored data at any time with the "Reset Progress" option on the Profile page, or by uninstalling the app.

5. This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics, and only remembers your language choice in your own browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receive your IP address to deliver the pages.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the app evolves. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

For questions or concerns, write to gianni85g@hotmail.it.
